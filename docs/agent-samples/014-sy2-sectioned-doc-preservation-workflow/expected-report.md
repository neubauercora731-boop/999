# Expected Report Behavior

The report content must be Chinese and must fit the original template's answer areas.

- The original teacher content remains unchanged.
- Code-related content is filled under `实验代码`.
- The real run screenshot and its explanation are filled under `实验结果与分析`.
- Reflection text is filled under `问题及思考`.
- The delivered DOCX contains concise labels such as `【代码】`, `【运行截图】`, and `【问题及思考】`.
- The delivered DOCX must not contain the words `系统填写`.

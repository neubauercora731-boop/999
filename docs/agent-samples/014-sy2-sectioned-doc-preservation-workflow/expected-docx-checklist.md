# DOCX Checklist

- Original teacher-provided content is preserved.
- Original markers remain present: `实验代码`, `实验结果与分析`, and `问题及思考`.
- Original requirement text remains present, including `一、实验要求`.
- The final DOCX contains a real screenshot image in `word/media`.
- The final DOCX contains `【代码】`.
- The final DOCX contains `【运行截图】`.
- The final DOCX contains `【问题及思考】`.
- The final DOCX does not contain the words `系统填写`.
- The final DOCX does not silently fall back to a freshly rewritten report.
